# Helix — Tester Setup Guide 🧬

Thanks for testing Helix! It's a dating app that matches on **genetic compatibility** —
photos stay blurred until you match *and* you're genetically compatible.

Follow these steps, and read **"How to actually get a match"** at the end — that's
the part everyone misses.

---

## 1. Install
- Tap the APK I sent you → allow **"Install from unknown sources"** if asked → Install.
- **First screen may take ~30–60 seconds** the first time — the server wakes from
  sleep. This is normal; it's fast after that.

## 2. Create your account
- **Sign up** with email (or **Continue with Google**).
- **Tick all three consent boxes** (health data · storage · 18+). The button stays
  greyed until you do.

## 3. Set your health profile ⚠️ *(most important — matching needs this)*
- Go to **Profile → edit your genotype & blood group** (or use **Scan** to read a
  lab report).
- Pick your **genotype** (AA / AS / SS / AC / SC) and blood group. Without this,
  compatibility can't be calculated.

## 4. Set your dating profile
- On **Profile**, set: **date of birth** (18+), **gender**, **looking for**,
  **city**, and a short bio. Add a **photo** if you like.

## 5. Discover & like
- Go to the **Discover** tab → **♥ like** people you're interested in, ✕ to pass.
- Use the **filter icon** (top-right) to narrow by **age** or **city**.

## 6. Match & chat
- When **two people like each other**, it's a **match** — it appears in **Matches**.
- If you're **genetically compatible**, the photo **unblurs**. If it's **higher
  risk**, the photo stays blurred with a short educational advisory.
- Tap a match to **chat**. 💬

---

## 🔑 How to actually get a match (why it says "No matches yet")

A match needs **two testers who like *each other***. So:

1. **Get at least 2–3 people signed up** and through steps 3–4 above.
2. **Set "Looking for" to *Everyone*** while testing — that way gender preferences
   never hide anyone from you.
3. Everyone opens **Discover** and **likes** each other.
4. The moment two of you have liked each other → **match** appears in Matches.

> You won't see someone in Discover if: you already liked/matched/blocked them,
> their profile isn't finished, or your "looking for" preferences don't line up.
